# User Manual: Command Center v2.1

## 🏠 Main Dashboard
The Dashboard provides a 360-degree view of HSE performance.
*   **KPI Cards:** Real-time counters for Total Incidents, Open Cases, and Training Compliance.
*   **Trend Charts:** Visual tracking of incident frequency over the last 6 months. Use this to identify emerging safety risks.
*   **Recent Feed:** A chronological stream of the latest site activities.

## 📝 Incident Management
### 1. Incident Register
*   **Search/Filter:** Use the top navigation bar to filter by **Site**, **Severity**, or **Status**.
*   **Color Coding:** 
    *   🔴 **Critical/High:** Immediate investigation required.
    *   🟡 **Medium:** Standard investigation workflow.
    *   🔵 **Low/Near-Miss:** Lesson-learned documentation.

### 2. Reporting an Incident
To log a new safety event:
1.  Navigate to **Report Incident**.
2.  **Section 1:** Enter date, time, and site location.
3.  **Section 2:** Classify the incident (e.g., LTI, First Aid) and assign a severity level.
4.  **Section 3:** Provide a detailed narrative (min 30 characters).
5.  **Submit:** The record is committed instantly to the database and appears in the Register.

## 🎓 Training Matrix
The Training Matrix tracks employee readiness across the Tilenga and Kingfisher projects.
*   **Compliance Status:**
    *   ✅ **Valid:** Certification is current.
    *   ❌ **Expired:** Employee is non-compliant and may need to be restricted from high-risk tasks.
*   **Proactive Monitoring:** Use the "Days Until Expiry" column to schedule recertification before lapses occur.

## 🚪 Secure Access
*   **Login:** Always use your assigned corporate credentials.
*   **Sign Out:** For security, always sign out before leaving your terminal. The system automatically terminates your session upon logout.
